Money Advisor 550 Uploader

Money Advisor 550 Uploader is a small publishing tool built for the owner of the Money Advisor 550 finance-education channel. It posts the owner's own short finance videos to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

The app is used by a single user — the channel owner — to publish their own content. It does not post on behalf of anyone else, does not collect other users' data, and does not sell or share data with third parties.
