Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026.

"Money Advisor 550 Uploader" (the "App") is a single-user publishing tool that posts the channel owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

1. Data the App handles

  • Account connection data: the TikTok account identifier and access token needed to authenticate publishing requests through TikTok's official APIs.
  • Content you provide: the video files, titles, descriptions, and hashtags you choose to publish. These are sent to TikTok only to complete the post you requested.

The App does not collect data about other TikTok users, does not track visitors to this support site, and does not use cookies or analytics on this site.

2. How data is used

Account and content data are used only to authenticate with TikTok and to publish the posts you request. Nothing is used for advertising.

3. Sharing

Data is shared only with TikTok, as required to publish your posts through its official APIs. Data is never sold, rented, or shared with any other third party.

4. Retention

Access tokens are kept only while the App remains connected to your TikTok account. Video files you provide are handled for the duration of the upload and are not kept longer than needed to complete or retry a post. You may disconnect the App at any time through TikTok's settings, which revokes its access.

5. Security

Access credentials are stored securely and are never shown to anyone. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but reasonable measures are used to protect them.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the TikTok Developer Portal, where the App is registered as "Money Advisor 550 Uploader".
